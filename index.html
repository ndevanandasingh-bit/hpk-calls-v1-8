HPK Calls V1.10.2 — Easy Connect

Make simple app-to-app voice or video calls.

  1. Enter your name and the contact name.
  2. Choose Voice or Video.
  3. Tap Start Call.
  4. HPK Calls creates the Room ID automatically and prepares the Room ID + join link for sharing immediately.
  5. The receiver taps the shared link and then taps Answer. You can also enter the Room ID manually.

If the internet changes briefly during a connected call, HPK Calls keeps the call open and tries to recover automatically.

Recent Calls now has one-tap redial using the previous Voice/Video mode.

If your browser does not allow the share sheet to open automatically, HPK Calls copies the Room ID for you and shows one large Share Call button.

Important: a browser cannot silently choose a WhatsApp/SMS recipient or send a message without your approval.
